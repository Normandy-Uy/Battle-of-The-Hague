Information we do not collect on our servers

We do not operate a player account system. We do not require email signup or social login. We do not run our own backend that receives your name, photo, or gameplay data for storage on our servers.

Information stored on your device

The App may store data locally on your device only, for example:

  • Game progress and unlock status
  • Audio / settings preferences
  • An optional display name you enter for the victory / “Dutz is free” selfie share image
  • An optional photo you choose for that same local selfie image

That optional name and photo are used in-app to compose a shareable image on your device. They are retained on the device (until you clear app data or uninstall) and are not uploaded by us to a Battle of The Hague server. If you share the image yourself through another app (for example a messaging or social app), that sharing is controlled by you and that other app’s privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show rewarded ads, including when you restart after defeat. Google and its partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising ID / device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • Device and app information
  • Ad interaction and performance data

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App may show Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog so you can choose whether to allow personalized ads. You can change that choice later using Privacy and cookie settings inside the App.

This advertising data is processed by Google under Google’s policies. See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy

You can reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device settings (Google settings → Ads, wording may vary by Android version).

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under 13.

Your choices

  • You may skip or leave blank optional selfie registration where the App allows it
  • You may clear local App data or uninstall the App to remove locally stored name, photo, progress, and settings
  • You may limit ad personalization / Advertising ID in Android settings
  • You may reopen ad consent choices from Privacy and cookie settings in the App
