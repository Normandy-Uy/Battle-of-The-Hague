HONEST ASSESSMENT:
The project is partially optimized for phones — Very Low quality forcing, shadow
kill, mip limits, and hippie pooling are real wins — but it is not a well-optimized
Android title yet. Authoring still ships a character-heavy Built-in scene with
zero LODs, no occlusion, OpenGL-only, and main-thread rendering. That mismatch
is why budget phones choke while flagships feel fine.

SINGLE BIGGEST FACTOR LIMITING PERFORMANCE:
Skinned NPC/giant mesh count without LOD or distance culling.
