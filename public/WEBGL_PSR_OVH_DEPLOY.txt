TROUBLESHOOT
------------
- Blank page: check nginx mime.types has application/wasm
- 404 on Build/*.wasm: confirm files under /var/www/psr/play/Build/
- Re-deploy: run .\deploy-1\deploy.ps1 again (backs up old /play on server)
